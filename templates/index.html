WR Probability — how the rating becomes a percentage

Every WR badge on the site is a calibrated cover probability: the rating (a points checklist) passed through a fit of how often each rating has actually cashed. Football (CFB, NFL) and futbol (EPL, UCL) each get their own fit, trained only on their own settled, deduplicated picks, scored on the rating frozen at submission, and re-fit automatically as results come in.
