War Room vs WR AI Test

The WR AI Test card is a duplicate of the football War Room card with one extra input: each source pick's argument, read and graded 0–10 by the model and frozen on the pick. An aligned side is vetoed when every graded argument on it reads as a story (3/10 or under), so the model can veto a story two cards agree on; the solo threshold and tie-breaks use the test score. WR Probability — how the rating becomes a percentage

Every WR badge on the site is a calibrated cover probability: the rating (a points checklist) passed through a fit of how often each rating has actually cashed. Football (CFB, NFL) and futbol (EPL, UCL) each get their own fit, trained only on their own settled, deduplicated picks and board leans (leans carry their own term in the fit, so a lean's probability is never confused with a pick's), scored on the rating frozen at kickoff (the last pregame capture; picks graded before kickoff ratings existed fall back to their submission rating), and re-fit automatically as results come in.
